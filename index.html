Paste the suspicious email

Paste the message text here. If you can, include the email headers too (the lines at the top such as From:, Reply-To: and Authentication-Results:). Headers allow extra checks; without them those checks are simply skipped.

Never click a suspicious link or open an attachment merely to test whether it is malicious.

How do I find an email's headers?

In Gmail: open the message, choose the three-dot menu, then Show original. In Outlook on the web: open the message, choose the three-dot menu, then View message source. In Outlook for Windows: open the message, choose File, then Properties. Copy the header text, paste it into the box above the email body, and leave one blank line in between.

Please read before relying on a result

This educational triage tool identifies patterns commonly associated with phishing and social engineering. It cannot prove that an email is malicious or safe. False positives and false negatives are possible. Verify important messages independently through official websites, applications, or known contact information.

This tool never visits links, never opens attachments, and never contacts any server. It only reads the text you paste, in your browser, and forgets it as soon as you clear the page or close the tab.
